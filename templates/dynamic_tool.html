Convert Images to Black & White Online

Convert color photographs and scans into crisp black-and-white images instantly in your browser without uploading your files to remote servers.

Practical Real-World Uses

  • Preparing Documents for Printing: Save color ink and toner by converting receipts, invoices, and study sheets to monochrome before printing.
  • Artistic Photography: Give family portraits, architectural shots, and street photography a classic, timeless black-and-white look.
  • Improving Text Readability: Strip color distractions and paper tints from scanned book pages and handwritten notes.

How to Use (3 Easy Steps)

  1. Select or Drop Image: Click the upload box or drag any JPG, PNG, WebP, or BMP file into your browser.
  2. Instant Preview: Your browser immediately converts the image to clean grayscale in real time.
  3. Download: Click "Download Result" to save your high-resolution black-and-white image with zero watermarks.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does converting to black and white reduce image file size?

Yes. Because monochrome images contain less color complexity, saving the result as PNG or compressed JPEG often reduces file sizes by 20% to 40%.

Will my image lose sharpness or resolution?

No. The original pixel dimensions, resolution, and geometric sharpness remain exactly the same as your source file.

Is my uploaded photograph saved on your servers?

Never. All pixel conversion happens inside your browser's local memory. Your photos never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why do my phone selfies appear flipped?

Most front-facing phone cameras display a mirror preview to make framing natural, but save the un-mirrored raw capture. Our horizontal flip tool mirrors the file back to how you saw it in the preview.

Will rotating cut off or crop the corners of my image?

No. When rotating by 90°, 180°, or 270°, the canvas automatically recalculates its width and height to fit the full rotated dimensions without clipping any content.

Does this process degrade image quality?

No. 90-degree rotations and horizontal or vertical flips are mathematically exact transpositions that preserve full image resolution without compression blur.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can increasing contrast help read faint scanned documents?

Yes. Increasing contrast pushes gray background paper toward pure white and faint pencil or ink marks toward dark black, making text much easier to read.

What is the difference between brightness and contrast?

Brightness shifts all pixels lighter or darker uniformly, whereas contrast increases the difference between dark shadows and bright highlights.

Is my file kept private?
