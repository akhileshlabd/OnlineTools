Client-Side AES Cryptographic Pipeline

Frequently Asked Questions

Can anyone open my PDF without the password?

No. The internal PDF stream data is mathematically scrambled. Without the correct password, PDF viewers cannot render the document pages.

Do you retain or recover lost passwords?

Because encryption happens strictly on your device and no data is sent to our servers, we have zero record of your password. Be sure to record your password securely.

Can I remove the password later?

Yes, if you possess the valid password, you can decrypt and remove protection using our PDF Unlock tool at any time.
