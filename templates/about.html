About Free Qube

Free Qube is a clean, practical web utility platform built to provide fast, privacy-focused online tools for everyday document, image, audio, and calculation tasks.

Our Mission: Client-Side First Privacy

In modern digital workflows, everyday operations—such as combining PDF contracts, resizing photos, formatting JSON payloads, or running loan calculations—should not require handing over personal documents to unknown remote servers. Traditional web converters upload your files to remote cloud storage where they can linger in temporary caches.

Free Qube takes a client-side first approach. Most tools run 100% locally in your browser using modern WebAssembly, HTML5 Canvas, and Web Workers—meaning your files stay on your device for immediate, private processing. For the few specialized features requiring server processing (such as text-to-speech audio rendering or complex document conversions), files are encrypted via HTTPS and deleted immediately after conversion.

Core Utility Categories

Our platform provides straightforward, browser-executed utilities across essential categories:

  • PDF Tools: Merge multiple PDFs into one organized document, extract or split pages, and secure files with passwords locally in your browser.
  • Image Optimization: Resize photos for web forms, convert between JPG, PNG, and WebP, remove photo backgrounds, and generate QR codes.
  • Financial Calculators: Plan your finances with clear, interactive calculators for SIP mutual fund growth, loan EMI amortizations, and compound interest.
  • Developer Utilities: Format and validate JSON, check code differences, encode or decode Base64, and generate MD5 or SHA cryptographic hashes safely.
  • Everyday Math & Health: Calculate percentages, sales discounts, chronological age, daily caloric intake, and Body Mass Index (BMI).
  • Network Diagnostics: Check your public IP address, test your download speed, and view basic browser and device information.

Our Commitment to User Privacy

Privacy is central to how we design every tool at Free Qube:

  • Client-Side First Processing: Most tools process files directly inside your browser memory using WebAssembly. Your files stay on your device. For tools that require server processing, files are transferred over secure HTTPS connections and deleted immediately after processing.
  • Zero Registration Barriers: No accounts, no email capture, and no phone verification. Every utility is immediately accessible to everyone.
  • Clean, Unwatermarked Outputs: We never inject watermarks, branding stamps, or promotional overlays into your exported documents or images.

Editorial Integrity & Transparency

Beyond our interactive utilities, our editorial blog publishes deeply researched, educational guides on data privacy, consumer technology, and web standards. We are dedicated to providing transparent, authentic information to empower users in their digital lives.
