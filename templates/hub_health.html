Precision Health & Metabolic Planning: Scientific Calculators

Proactive wellness, athletic performance conditioning, and nutritional planning require accurate physiological calculations. Free Qube's Health & Fitness Calculators Suite delivers instant, scientifically validated computational models to help you understand your metabolic rate, body composition benchmarks, and gestational timelines.

Scientific Formulas & Physiological Models

  • Body Mass Index (BMI): Evaluates body mass relative to height using World Health Organization (WHO) classification standards: \(BMI = \frac{\text{weight (kg)}}{\text{height (m)}^2}\).
  • Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE): Combines Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) derived from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation with validated physical activity multipliers to establish exact caloric equilibrium.
  • Calorie Deficit & Surplus Targets: Calculates sustainable caloric adjustments (typically 300–500 kcal/day) for fat loss or muscle hypertrophy without compromising metabolic health.
  • Pregnancy Due Date Estimator: Implements Naegele’s rule based on the first day of the last menstrual period (LMP) with standard 280-day gestational pacing.

100% In-Browser Privacy for Biometric Data

Biometric metrics—including body weight, age, and pregnancy dates—are among the most sensitive personal data points you enter online. Unlike mobile apps that monetize health metrics through ad retargeting, Free Qube executes all physiological algorithms locally in your browser memory. Your biometrics are never saved, tracked, or sent across any network.
