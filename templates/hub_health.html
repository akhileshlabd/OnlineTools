Simple, Private Health & Fitness Calculators for Daily Wellness

Setting realistic wellness goals, planning daily nutrition, or preparing for a new baby starts with clear, reliable numbers. Free Qube's Health & Fitness Calculators give you instant estimates for body composition, calorie expenditure, and key health milestones without requiring app downloads or account logins.

Practical Everyday Health Planning

  • Body Mass Index (BMI): Quickly check where your weight falls relative to your height using standardized World Health Organization (WHO) categories (underweight, normal weight, overweight, or obese).
  • Daily Calorie Needs & TDEE: Calculate your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) based on your age, sex, height, weight, and activity level to build a sensible diet plan for weight loss, maintenance, or muscle gain.
  • Calorie Deficit or Surplus: Estimate the right calorie adjustment (typically 300 to 500 calories a day) to lose fat gradually or build strength sustainably.
  • Pregnancy Due Date Estimator: Estimate your baby's expected arrival date and track your current pregnancy trimester based on your last menstrual period.

How to Use the Health Calculators (3 Easy Steps)

  1. Select Your Tool: Choose the calculator you need above (BMI, Calorie Tracker, TDEE, or Pregnancy Due Date).
  2. Input Your Details: Enter your basic measurements (such as height, weight, age, or date) using metric or imperial units.
  3. Review Your Personalized Results: Get an instant breakdown of your numbers along with practical guidelines to discuss with your doctor or trainer.

Client-Side First Privacy

Client-Side First Privacy: Most tools run 100% locally in your browser using WebAssembly—your files stay on your device. For the few specialized features requiring server processing, files are encrypted via HTTPS and deleted immediately after conversion. Because your health measurements, weight, and family dates are evaluated entirely inside your browser's local memory, your personal health information is never saved, tracked, or sold to advertising networks.
