How does our Private AI Background Remover work?

Traditional background removal tools require you to upload your photos to a remote server. This compromises your privacy and costs the developer money, which is why they usually charge you a subscription.

Free Qube uses modern Web Machine Learning (WebML). When you use this tool, your browser downloads a highly optimized, quantized neural network (only ~40MB) directly into your local cache. Your device's own CPU and GPU then process the image locally to perfectly isolate the subject and remove the background.

The Result? Absolute privacy. Your image never touches our servers. And because we don't have to pay for expensive cloud GPUs, we can offer this premium AI tool to you 100% free forever.
