Free Online PDF Tools for Everyday Work and Study

PDFs are the standard format for resumes, legal agreements, tax forms, and school assignments because they keep the same layout on any screen. Free Qube provides simple, fast tools to organize, convert, and protect your PDF documents without installing software or paying monthly subscriptions.

Client-Side First Privacy Architecture

Client-Side First Privacy: Most tools run 100% locally in your browser using WebAssembly—your files stay on your device. For the few specialized features requiring server processing, files are encrypted via HTTPS and deleted immediately after conversion. We never store, read, or inspect your documents.

Practical Real-World Uses

  • Merge Documents for Email: Combine multiple scanned receipts, invoices, or cover letters into one neat PDF attachment instead of sending separate files.
  • Extract & Split Pages: Pull out only the relevant pages from a long handbook or contract to share just what is needed.
  • Password Protection: Lock sensitive financial statements or medical forms with strong password encryption before sending them.
  • Convert Office Files: Turn Microsoft Word (.docx) or Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheets into clean, print-ready PDFs that anyone can open.
  • Image to PDF: Pack several photos or receipt snapshots into a single tidy PDF file in seconds.

No Watermarks & No Hidden Fees

All tools on Free Qube are completely free to use. We never stamp watermarks onto your finished files, and there are no daily limits or sign-up walls. For practical tips on keeping file sizes small, read our guide on Merging Large PDFs Quickly in Your Browser.
