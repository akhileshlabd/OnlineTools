Choosing the Right Image Format

Not sure which format to choose? Here is a quick guide:

  • JPG (JPEG): Best for photographs and images with complex colors. It uses lossy compression, which means you can significantly reduce the file size, making it ideal for email and web pages.
  • PNG: Best for graphics, logos, and images requiring a transparent background. PNG uses lossless compression, meaning no quality is lost, but the file sizes are generally larger than JPGs.
  • WEBP: The modern standard for web images. Developed by Google, WEBP provides superior compression (both lossy and lossless) compared to JPG and PNG, making your websites load much faster.

By adjusting the quality slider (available for JPG and WEBP), you can compress the image further. A quality setting of 80-85% is generally the sweet spot for maintaining visual fidelity while drastically reducing the file size.
