Our Support Policy & SLA Commitment

At Free Qube, we pride ourselves on delivering 100% free, client-side utility tools. Because all tools process files locally within your device's memory using modern WebAssembly and JavaScript runtimes (without uploading document bytes to our servers), our team does not have access to your private files, spreadsheets, or images.

Troubleshooting Client-Side Processing Issues

If you experience an issue or memory error with a specific file, please follow these standard troubleshooting guidelines before contacting support:

  • Browser Compatibility: Ensure you are using the latest version of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari, or Microsoft Edge with WebAssembly and JavaScript enabled.
  • Memory Allocation: Large multimedia files or massive spreadsheets require sufficient device RAM. If a tab crashes during conversion, close background applications or split large files.
  • Hardware Acceleration: For video and image processing tools utilizing WebGL or Canvas, ensure hardware acceleration is toggled on in your browser settings.

Business Inquiries, Editorial Feedback & Bug Reporting
