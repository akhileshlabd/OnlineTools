How to Use the Free PDF Splitter

Splitting a large PDF document into smaller, more manageable pieces is quick and easy with Free Qube. First, select the PDF file you want to split by clicking the upload area or dragging and dropping the file into the box. Once your file is uploaded, enter the specific page numbers or ranges you wish to extract (for example, "1-3, 5, 7"). Click the split button, and your new document containing only those pages will be generated instantly.

Why Split PDFs?

There are many reasons you might need to split a PDF document:

  • Email Attachments: Large PDF files often exceed email attachment size limits. Splitting the document allows you to send only the most relevant pages.
  • Organization: Extract specific chapters, invoices, or reports from a massive master document for better digital filing.
  • Privacy: Remove sensitive or confidential pages from a document before sharing it with third parties.

Secure and Private Processing

Your privacy is our top priority. When you split a PDF using our tool, the file is securely transmitted over an encrypted connection. As soon as the split is complete and you download your new file, our servers automatically and permanently delete both the original file and the newly generated document. We do not keep logs of your data, and we do not apply any watermarks to your extracted pages.
