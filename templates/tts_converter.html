Convert Text to Speech and Download MP3s

Our free online Text to Speech (TTS) converter transforms any written text into natural, lifelike speech instantly. Whether you are generating voiceovers for YouTube videos, creating study materials, or building accessibility features, this tool gives you granular control over vocal pitch, speed, and emotion. Because it runs 100% locally using your browser's native Web Speech API, there are absolutely no server delays, hidden costs, or data privacy risks.

How to Use the TTS Converter

  1. Enter your text: Paste up to 5,000 characters into the input box above.
  2. Choose a voice: Select from a list of high-quality natural voices provided by your operating system (Google, Apple, Microsoft).
  3. Tweak the tone: Use the Quick Emotion Presets (Excited, Calm, Boss) or manually adjust the Pitch and Speed sliders to perfect the vocal delivery.
  4. Preview and Download: Click "Play" to test the voice. To save it, click "Capture & Download". (Note: You may be prompted to share your tab's audio to allow the browser to record the system stream).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the MP3 download free for commercial use?

Yes, our tool is completely free. However, the commercial rights to the actual voices depend on your operating system (e.g., Apple macOS voices vs Microsoft Windows natural voices). Most built-in OS voices are free for standard content creation.

Why do I need to "Share Tab Audio" to download?

For strict security reasons, modern web browsers do not allow websites to silently download system voice data. To generate an audio file securely on your own device, we use the WebRTC MediaRecorder API to listen to the tab's output. This guarantees your data never leaves your computer.

Can I generate audio in other languages?

Absolutely! The Voice Dropdown automatically populates with every language pack installed on your device (Spanish, French, Japanese, etc.).
