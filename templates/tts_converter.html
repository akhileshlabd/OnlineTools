Acoustic Synthesis Architecture

Text normalization and phoneme tokenization occur prior to waveform synthesis. Input strings are parsed into contextual clauses, punctuation marks are mapped to cadence intervals, and acoustic pitch contours are rendered using multi-lingual neural synthesis engines.
