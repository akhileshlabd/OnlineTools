Free Online Sudoku: Rules, Strategies & Algorithmic Solving

Sudoku is an internationally celebrated logic-based combinatorial number-placement puzzle. The classic objective is to populate a standard 9×9 grid with digits from 1 through 9 such that each column, each row, and each of the nine 3×3 subgrids (commonly referred to as blocks, regions, or boxes) contains all digits from 1 to 9 without repetition.

Algorithmic Architecture & Seeded Generation

Unlike legacy puzzle sites that rely on static database queries or repetitive puzzle packs, Free Qube executes a procedural backtracking solver paired with a deterministic Mulberry32 pseudorandom number generator (PRNG) directly inside your client browser. A fully compliant Latin square grid is constructed algorithmically. Individual cells are then strategically removed according to selected difficulty parameters (Easy, Medium, Hard, or Expert) while rigorously verifying that exactly one unique logical solution exists. Processing is 100% client-side, enabling zero latency and seamless offline puzzle generation.

Step-by-Step Playing Guide

  1. Select Difficulty: Choose from Easy (ideal for beginners), Medium, Hard, or Expert to adjust the number of pre-filled clues.
  2. Identify Candidates: Click any empty cell to activate row, column, and block cross-highlighting. Toggle Notes mode to jot down pencil candidates.
  3. Input Numbers: Tap the digital numeric keypad or press numbers 1–9 on your physical keyboard to place digits.
  4. Review & Correct: Invalid entries violating Sudoku constraints are highlighted in red instantly. Use Erase or Hint when encountering complex deductions.
