Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026

At Free Qube (accessible from our website), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Free Qube and how we use it. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.

1. File Processing & Document Privacy

We understand that the files you process using our PDF, Image, and Resume tools may contain sensitive personal or business information. Free Qube is designed with your privacy in mind:

  • Browser-Side Processing: Whenever possible, our tools process your files locally within your web browser. In these cases, your files are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Server-Side Processing: For certain complex operations that require server resources, your files are securely transmitted to our servers via encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Automatic Deletion: Any files uploaded to our servers for processing are temporary. They are strictly used to fulfill your requested task and are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers shortly after processing is complete. We do not keep backups, and we do not view, analyze, or share the contents of your files.

2. Log Files

Free Qube follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this and a part of hosting services' analytics. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Free Qube uses 'cookies'. These cookies are used to store information including visitors' preferences, and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the users' experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and/or other information.

4. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie and AdSense

Google is one of a third-party vendor on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. However, visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL – https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Some of our advertising partners may use cookies and web beacons on our site. Our advertising partners include Google AdSense. These third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Free Qube, which are sent directly to users' browser. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that Free Qube has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. Third Party Privacy Policies

Free Qube's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. Thus, we are advising you to consult the respective Privacy Policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information. It may include their practices and instructions about how to opt-out of certain options. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its Terms and Conditions. This policy applies only to our online activities and is valid for visitors to our website with regards to the information that they shared and/or collect in Free Qube.
