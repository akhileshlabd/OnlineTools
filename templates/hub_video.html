Simple, Fast Online Video Tools Without Cloud Uploads

Extracting audio from a recorded lecture or removing background noise from a video clip shouldn't require waiting in long upload queues or paying for heavy video editing software. Free Qube's Video Tools process your media locally inside your web browser with zero watermarks and complete privacy.

Practical Real-World Use Cases

  • Extracting MP3 Audio Tracks: Rip clear audio tracks from recorded webinars, classroom lectures, conference presentations, or music performances to listen on the go or create study podcasts.
  • Muting Video Clips: Strip noisy wind rumble, traffic sounds, or private background conversations before sharing clips on social media (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts) or adding them to corporate slides.
  • Data & Bandwidth Savings: Process multi-gigabyte video clips directly on your machine without consuming limited mobile data caps or waiting for gigabytes of uploads.
  • Lossless Video Quality: When removing audio, our tool strips the sound track directly without re-encoding the video frames, preserving 100% of the original video resolution and sharpness.

How to Use the Video Tools (3 Easy Steps)

  1. Select Your Tool: Choose "Video to MP3" or "Video Muter" from the cards above.
  2. Drop Your Video File: Drag and drop your MP4, MOV, or WebM file into the browser workspace.
  3. Process & Download: Let your device process the file locally in seconds and click Download to save your final MP3 audio or muted video.

Client-Side First Privacy

Client-Side First Privacy: Most tools run 100% locally in your browser using WebAssembly—your files stay on your device. For the few specialized features requiring server processing, files are encrypted via HTTPS and deleted immediately after conversion. By using WebAssembly, video demuxing and audio extraction run right on your machine's CPU/GPU. Your private home videos, conference recordings, and personal media files are never transferred across the internet or stored on external hard drives.
