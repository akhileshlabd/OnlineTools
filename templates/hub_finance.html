Simple, Accurate Financial Calculators for Everyday Planning

Planning for long-term investments, comparing loan options, or calculating monthly budgets shouldn't require complex spreadsheets. Free Qube's Financial Calculators help you make informed money decisions with clear projections and visual charts—completely free and without sign-ups.

Practical Everyday Financial Planning

  • Monthly SIP Mutual Funds: Plan your monthly investments into mutual funds or index funds. Visualize how steady, disciplined monthly contributions can grow into substantial wealth over 5, 10, or 20 years.
  • Home Loan & Personal Loan EMI: Calculate exact monthly loan installments before speaking to a bank. See how much of each payment goes toward paying down principal balance versus interest charges.
  • Compound Interest (CAGR): Understand the compounding growth of your savings or investments over time when returns are reinvested quarterly, monthly, or annually.
  • Fixed Deposit (FD) & Lumpsum Growth: Estimate guaranteed maturity values on term deposits and one-time investments with precise maturity dates.

How to Use the Calculators (3 Easy Steps)

  1. Select Your Calculator: Click on any calculator above, such as SIP, EMI, or Compound Interest.
  2. Enter Your Numbers: Type in your starting amount, expected annual return or interest rate, and total years (tenure).
  3. Review Instant Results: See your total interest, total maturity wealth, and visual breakdown charts immediately as you move the sliders.

Client-Side First Privacy

Client-Side First Privacy: Most tools run 100% locally in your browser using WebAssembly—your files stay on your device. For the few specialized features requiring server processing, files are encrypted via HTTPS and deleted immediately after conversion. All arithmetic calculations happen directly in your browser's memory, meaning your salary, loans, and portfolio numbers are never saved or shared with third parties.
