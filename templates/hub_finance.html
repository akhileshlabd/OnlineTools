Core Principles of Compounding & Amortization

Our calculators model real-world financial vehicles by evaluating double-precision arithmetic in client-side memory:

  • Systematic Investment Plans (SIP): Evaluates regular recurring capital deployment into equity mutual funds via annuity accumulation: \(M = P \times \frac{(1 + i)^n - 1}{i} \times (1 + i)\).
  • Compound Interest (CAGR): Demonstrates how reinvesting yields across quarterly, semi-annual, or monthly intervals exponentially outpaces simple linear interest over multi-decade horizons.
  • Equated Monthly Installments (EMI): Computes exact monthly debt obligations using reducing-balance amortization schedules, distinguishing interest payments from principal reduction.
  • Fixed Deposit (FD): Projects guaranteed-return banking certificates across multi-year maturity horizons.
