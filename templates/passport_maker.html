How to Create Passport Photos Free Online

Creating a compliant 2x2 inch passport or ID photo usually requires a trip to the pharmacy or paying for expensive software. Our free Smart Passport Photo Maker automates the entire process using advanced Artificial Intelligence. Simply upload a selfie or portrait photo, and our AI will automatically remove the background, detect your facial landmarks, center your face, and scale it to meet strict government requirements (ensuring your head takes up 50-70% of the vertical frame).

100% Secure Client-Side Micro-AI

Because passport photos contain sensitive biometric data, privacy is paramount. Unlike other online tools that upload your face to a remote server, Free Qube utilizes a revolutionary Zero Server Cost Architecture. The AI models (Google's MediaPipe Task Vision API) are downloaded securely to your browser's local cache. All background removal, mask anti-aliasing, and face detection happen entirely on your own device's CPU/GPU. Your photos never leave your computer.

Ready for 4x6 Printing

Once your portrait is perfectly extracted and placed onto a crisp white background, the tool automatically generates a high-resolution 300 DPI sheet formatted for standard 4x6 inch photo paper. The grid fits exactly six 2x2 inch photos, complete with subtle crop marks. You can download the high-res JPG and print it at any local photo center for cents, saving you time and money.
